# Verified Vehicle Training and HART Batch 3

This Stage 36A batch converts the official UK course matrix into structured vehicle requirements and records official specialist-building compatibility.

## Coverage

- Fire Academy: HazMat, Mobile command and ARFF-Training;
- Rescue (EMS) Academy: Critical care, HART Training, Tactical Command Course, SORT Training and Ambulance Officer;
- Police training centre: Police aviation, Firearms training, Dog handling, Roads Policing Officer Training and Mounted Training;
- HART Base, Small Ambulance Station, Helicopter Station, Police Helicopter Station and Large Police Depot compatibility.

## Evidence boundary

The batch records only explicit official course-to-vehicle and building-to-vehicle relationships. It does not infer vehicle prices, staffing or a training requirement for welfare vehicles; official release history states that the HART training requirement was removed from welfare vehicles.
